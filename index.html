How to use

A simple routine

Use them in this order. Consistency brings beauty, so give your skin a few weeks.

  1. 1

    Tone

    Morning & night

    Sweep Pure Touch toner over clean skin to hydrate and prep.

  2. 2

    Mask

    2–3 times a week

    Mix the Whitening Pack into a paste, apply, rinse when dry.

  3. 3

    Moisturise

    Every day

    Smooth Bright Blast lotion over arms, legs and body.

  4. 4

    Repair

    Before bed

    Finish with a thin layer of Bright Blast night cream.
